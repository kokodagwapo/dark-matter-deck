It was not always that safe. In aviation's early decades, crashes were common, and the fatal-accident rate was many times higher than it is today. The industry chose to change. Safety became a discipline, built on shared standards, consistent data, and real accountability for every crew and every flight.

Narration
That discipline is codified. The FAA's airline transport pilot standards define a stable approach in exact numbers. Before final approach: altitude within one hundred feet, heading within five degrees, airspeed within ten knots. From the final approach fix to decision height, airspeed within five knots and guidance within a quarter scale. At decision height, the required visual references are there, or the pilot executes a missed approach. No exceptions.
